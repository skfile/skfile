# Savik Kinger

I'm a PhD student in NeuroAI at Yale, working on causal inference, manifold learning, and theoretical neuroscience. I use mathematics and computational tools to study how brains and artificial systems learn, and what their structure can tell us about how they work.

Previously, I studied computer science and pure mathematics at Columbia University.

[Website](https://skfile.github.io/) · [Writing](https://mindmesh.substack.com/)

## Selected projects

- **[Neural Trajectory Explorer](https://github.com/skfile/neural-trajectory-explorer)** — Interactive visualizations of neural population activity, trajectories, and encoding manifolds.
- **[Geometric Separation](https://github.com/skfile/geometric-separation)** — Experiments on when manifold components remain distinct under different sampling and embedding methods.
- **[School Assignment Fairness](https://github.com/skfile/school-assignment-fairness)** — Exploring tradeoffs between travel distance, demographic balance, and school capacity through optimization.
- **[Aver](https://stackcert-testing.savikk129.workers.dev/early/console)** — An early demo for evaluating whether changes to an AI system meet the standards people expect of it.

I also write about technology, society, and the ideas I keep coming back to. Earlier projects and contribution forks remain available in my archived repositories.
